How to Play

📍 Phase 1: Placement

Players take turns placing their balls on empty vertices of the grid. Continue until all balls are placed.

🔄 Phase 2: Movement

Players take turns moving one ball to an adjacent empty vertex. Adjacent means connected by a grid line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).

  • Cannot jump over any balls
  • Cannot capture opponent's balls
  • Must move each turn

🏆 Victory

Form a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) with all N of your balls to win!
